# 图表作文模板

> 图表作文是议论文的"数据版"，重点在描述数据 + 给出原因。

## 🏗 四段式结构

### 第一段：描述图表（2-3 句）
- 图表类型 + 主题
- 关键数据（最高、最低、趋势）

### 第二段：解读原因（4-5 句）
- 原因 1 + 解释
- 原因 2 + 解释

### 第三段：评论 / 预测（2-3 句）
- 我的看法 / 影响
- 未来趋势预测

### 第四段：建议 / 总结（2-3 句）
- 措施 / 建议
- 展望

## 📈 图表类型与描述句型

### 1. 柱状图（Bar Chart）

**描述变化**：
> The number of [对象] **increased / rose / grew** significantly from [数字] in [年份] to [数字] in [年份].
> The figure **doubled** / **tripled** during the period.

**描述对比**：
> [类别 A] is **twice as high as** [类别 B].
> [类别 A] **accounts for** [比例] of the total, **ranking** first among all categories.

### 2. 折线图（Line Chart）

**趋势描述**：
> The trend **showed an upward / downward** tendency over the period.
> There was a **dramatic / steady / slight** increase / decrease in [指标].

**拐点描述**：
> The figure **reached its peak** in [年份].
> The number **bottomed out** in [年份].
> A **sharp decline** occurred between [年份] and [年份].

### 3. 饼图（Pie Chart）

> [类别] **occupies / accounts for / makes up** [比例] of the total.
> [类别] **ranks first / takes the largest share** in the chart.

### 4. 表格（Table）

> According to the table, [关键数据对比].
> The most striking feature is that [现象].

## 📝 完整模板

### 第一段：描述数据

```text
The chart / table clearly illustrates [主题] in [地点 / 时间]. 
According to the data, [对象 1] [动词] from [数字] in [年份] to [数字] in [年份], 
while [对象 2] [动词] from [数字] to [数字]. 
It is evident that [核心趋势].
```

### 第二段：分析原因

```text
Several factors may account for this phenomenon. 
First, [原因 1]. 
Second, [原因 2]. 
In addition, [原因 3]. 
```

### 第三段：评论 / 预测

```text
This trend, in my view, [评价]. 
If this tendency continues, [预测].
```

### 第四段：建议

```text
Therefore, it is essential that [建议 1]. 
Only by [措施] can we [目标].
```

## 🎯 数字表达必备

### 基础数字
- 30% — a / one third
- 50% — half / a half
- 75% — three quarters
- 25% — a quarter / one fourth

### 比例表达
- 占 X% — account for / make up / represent / constitute
- X 的两倍 — twice as ... as / double
- 比 X 高出 20% — 20% higher than

### 趋势动词
| 上升 | 下降 | 波动 |
|---|---|---|
| rise | fall | fluctuate |
| increase | decrease | vary |
| grow | decline | oscillate |
| soar | drop | undulate |
| surge | plunge | alternate |
| climb | sink |  |

## 📊 高分句型

### 描述阶段
> The number of [对象] **witnessed a dramatic increase** from X to Y during the period.
> [对象] **experienced a steady growth** over the past decade.

### 表达倍数
> The figure **doubled** in [year].
> It **tripled** the previous year's number.

### 表达最大 / 最小
> [类别] **ranked the highest**, accounting for [比例].
> [类别] **took the smallest share**, only [比例].

## ⚠️ 图表作文避坑

1. **不要罗列所有数据**——选 2-3 个关键点
2. **不要做"无评论描述"**——必须有原因分析
3. **不要照搬数据**——要转换表达方式
4. **不要忽略单位**——% / 倍 / 人数 / 金额要分清
5. **时态用一般现在时**——除非是过去某段时间的变化

## 💡 真题示例（2019-6）

> 某高校学生每天使用手机时间分布（饼图）：
> - 1 小时以下 10%
> - 1-3 小时 35%
> - 3-5 小时 30%
> - 5 小时以上 25%

**第一段**：
> The pie chart clearly illustrates the distribution of time college students spend on mobile phones daily. As can be seen from the chart, **only 10% of students use their phones for less than one hour per day**, while **35% spend 1-3 hours**. Notably, **students who use phones for more than 5 hours account for 25%**, indicating that excessive phone use is a common issue among college students.

**第二段**：
> Several factors contribute to this phenomenon. First, with the popularity of social media and short videos, students are easily drawn into their phones. Second, the lack of self-discipline also plays a role. In addition, some students use phones as a way to escape academic pressure.
