# 2021 年 12 月真题 - 段落匹配

**主题**：Multicultural Identity in Modern Society

## 📋 段落匹配解题套路

**步骤 1：先看题干，圈关键词**
每题 2-3 个关键词（人名、数字、专有名词、动词、形容词）。

**步骤 2：扫读每段首末句**
匹配题答案常在段落首末句或转折/举例处。

**步骤 3：同义替换**
- 原词 → 同义词
- 主动 → 被动
- 正话 → 反说
- 例子 → 概念

## 🎯 真题示例

**题干**：
- Q1: The definition of "cultural identity" varies among scholars.
- Q2: Globalization has both positive and negative effects on local cultures.
- Q3: **Studies show bilingual children have better cognitive flexibility.** ✓
- Q4: Many immigrants face identity crisis in their new country.
- Q5: Some countries adopt multicultural policies to address diversity.

**答案分布规律**（重要！）：
- 一般不会有连续两段都对应同一题
- 一段可能对应多题（特别是举例段）

## 📚 文化话题高频词

- cultural diversity 文化多样性
- ethnic minority 少数民族
- assimilation 同化
- integration 融合
- cosmopolitan 世界主义的
- heritage 遗产
- indigenous 本土的
- prejudice 偏见
- stereotype 刻板印象
- tolerance 包容

## 📝 长难句精选

> **While globalization has undeniably brought the world closer together**, it has also **given rise to a paradox** where the very forces that connect us can simultaneously amplify our differences.

**结构**：While 引导让步状语 + 主句 + where 定语从句 + that 定语从句

**翻译**：虽然全球化确实让世界变得更紧密，但它也造成了一个悖论——那些将我们联系在一起的力量同时也放大了我们的差异。

## 💡 写作素材

**议论文开头模板**：
> In contemporary society, the topic of [文化/教育/科技] has sparked heated debate. **While some hold the view that...**, others strongly **oppose / advocate** that...

**议论文结尾模板**：
> Taking all these factors into consideration, we may safely **draw the conclusion that**...
