# 2021 年 6 月真题 - 仔细阅读 Passage 1

**主题**：Remote Work: The Future of Employment?（远程办公：就业的未来？）

## 📰 文章概述

讨论 COVID-19 推动的远程办公浪潮，分析其对员工、企业、社会的长期影响。

**核心论点**：
1. 远程办公提升了 productivity 和 work-life balance
2. 但削弱了团队凝聚力和企业 culture
3. 未来将是 hybrid（混合办公）模式

## 🔍 考点分析

### 高频词
- **remote** /rɪˈmoʊt/ adj. 远程的、偏僻的
- **hybrid** /ˈhaɪbrɪd/ adj. 混合的
- **productivity** /ˌprɑːdʌkˈtɪvəti/ n. 生产力
- **collaboration** /kəˌlæbəˈreɪʃn/ n. 协作
- **commute** /kəˈmjuːt/ n./v. 通勤

### 长难句拆解

> **While the shift to remote work has been widely celebrated as a win for employee flexibility and well-being**, it has also raised concerns about the **erosion of spontaneous collaboration and mentorship opportunities** that traditionally occur in physical workplaces.

**结构分析**：
- 让步状语从句：While the shift to remote work has been widely celebrated...
- 主句：it has also raised concerns about...
- 同位语：the erosion of spontaneous collaboration and mentorship opportunities
- 定语从句：that traditionally occur in physical workplaces

**翻译**：虽然远程办公的转变被广泛赞誉为员工灵活性和福祉的胜利，但它也引发了人们对协作和指导机会流失的担忧——这些机会传统上发生在实体工作场所。

## 📝 应试技巧：主旨题

**题目 1**：What is the author's main point about remote work?

A. Remote work is the future of all companies.
B. Remote work has no benefits at all.
C. **Remote work has both advantages and disadvantages.** ✓
D. Companies should completely return to office.

**解析**：
- 作者态度关键："While... celebrated... it has also raised concerns..."
- 转折结构 "while... also" 表明作者持"双面"立场
- 这就是"客观陈述"型主旨题，对应 C
