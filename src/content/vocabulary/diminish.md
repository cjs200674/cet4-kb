# diminish

**释义**：减少；减小；贬低

**英文释义**：to make or become less; to make someone seem less important

**例句**：
- Her enthusiasm **diminished** over time.
- Don't **diminish** his achievements.

**搭配**：
- diminish in size / value 尺寸/价值减少
- diminish the importance of 贬低……的重要性

**辨析**：
- diminish 强调"逐渐减少"
- decrease 通用"减少"
- reduce "减少、降低"
- dwindle "逐渐减少至耗尽"

**记忆法**：`di-`(分开) + `mini`(小，源于 minor) + `-ish`(使) → 使变小 → 减少
