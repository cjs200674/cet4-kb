# assess

**释义**：评估；估价

**英文释义**：to evaluate or estimate the value of something

**例句**：
- Teachers need to **assess** students' progress.
- The damage was **assessed** at $10,000.

**搭配**：
- assess the situation 评估形势
- assess the impact 评估影响
- assess sb.'s ability 评估某人的能力

**派生**：
- assessment (n.) 评估
- assessor (n.) 评估员

**辨析**：
- assess "评估"，强调估值
- evaluate "评价"，更通用
- estimate "估计"，强调大致数字

**记忆法**：`as-`(朝向) + `sess`(坐，源于 session) + → 坐在旁边看 → 评估
