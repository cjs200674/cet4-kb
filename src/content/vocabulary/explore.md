# explore

**释义**：探索；勘探；探讨

**英文释义**：to travel through an area to learn about it; to examine carefully

**例句**：
- They **explored** the rainforest for two weeks.
- Let's **explore** different solutions.

**搭配**：
- explore space 探索太空
- explore ideas 探讨想法
- explore the possibility 探讨可能性

**派生**：
- exploration (n.) 探索
- explorer (n.) 探险家
- exploratory (adj.) 探索性的

**辨析**：
- explore "探索、勘探"
- discover "发现"
- investigate "调查"
- research "研究"

**记忆法**：`ex-`(出) + `plore`(喊，源于 deplore) → 喊出来 → 探索
