# alleviate

**释义**：减轻；缓和

**英文释义**：to make suffering or a problem less severe

**例句**：
- The medicine will **alleviate** the pain.
- We need to **alleviate** traffic congestion in the city.

**搭配**：
- alleviate pain / suffering 减轻痛苦
- alleviate poverty 缓解贫困
- alleviate stress 缓解压力

**辨析**：
- alleviate 主要指"减轻痛苦、压力"
- relieve "缓解、减轻"，较通用
- reduce "减少、降低"

**记忆法**：`al-`(加强) + `levi`(轻，源于 levity) + `-ate` → 使变轻 → 减轻
