# perceive

**释义**：感知；察觉；理解

**英文释义**：to become aware of something; to understand

**例句**：
- She **perceived** a change in his attitude.
- How do you **perceive** your role in the team?

**搭配**：
- perceive as 视为
- perceive sth. as a threat 把……视为威胁

**派生**：
- perception (n.) 感知、看法
- perceptive (adj.) 敏锐的
- perceptible (adj.) 可察觉的

**记忆法**：`per-`(通过) + `ceive`(拿，源于 receive) → 通过感官获得 → 感知
