# accumulate

**释义**：积累；积聚

**英文释义**：to gradually get more and more of something over a period of time

**例句**：
- Dust had **accumulated** on the books.
- He **accumulated** a fortune through hard work.

**搭配**：
- accumulate wealth / experience 积累财富/经验
- accumulate over time 随时间积累

**辨析**：
- accumulate 强调"逐渐积累"
- collect 强调"收集、采集"
- gather 强调"聚集、集合"

**记忆法**：`ac-`(加强) + `cumul`(堆，源于 cumulative) + `-ate`(动词后缀) → 不断堆积
