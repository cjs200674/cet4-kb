# reveal

**释义**：揭示；透露；显示

**英文释义**：to show something that was hidden; to make known

**例句**：
- The study **revealed** interesting patterns.
- She **revealed** her secret to me.

**搭配**：
- reveal a secret 透露秘密
- reveal the truth 揭示真相
- reveal itself 显现

**派生**：
- revelation (n.) 揭示、揭露
- revealing (adj.) 暴露的、揭露性的

**辨析**：
- reveal "揭示"，强调揭露
- expose "暴露、揭露"
- disclose "公开、透露"
- unveil "揭开、揭幕"

**记忆法**：`re-`(相反) + `veal`(面纱，源于 veil) → 揭开面纱 → 揭示
