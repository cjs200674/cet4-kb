# anticipate

**释义**：预期；预料；期待

**英文释义**：to expect or predict something

**例句**：
- We **anticipate** strong demand for the new product.
- I **anticipated** his arrival at noon.

**搭配**：
- anticipate doing sth. 期待做某事
- anticipate the consequences 预料后果

**派生**：
- anticipation (n.) 预期、期待
- anticipated (adj.) 预期的

**记忆法**：`anti-`(前) + `cip`(拿，取，源于 capture) + `-ate` → 提前拿走 → 预料
