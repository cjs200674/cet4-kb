# manipulate

**释义**：操纵；操作；篡改

**英文释义**：to control or use something skillfully; to influence unfairly

**例句**：
- He **manipulated** the data to support his theory.
- She knows how to **manipulate** people.

**搭配**：
- manipulate data 篡改数据
- manipulate sb. 操纵某人
- manipulate a tool 操作工具

**派生**：
- manipulation (n.) 操纵
- manipulative (adj.) 善于操纵的
- manipulator (n.) 操纵者

**辨析**：
- manipulate "操纵、操作"
- control "控制"
- handle "处理、操作"
- operate "操作、运转"

**记忆法**：`mani`(手) + `pul`(拉) + `-ate` → 用手拉 → 操作、操纵
