# appreciate

**释义**：欣赏；感激；领会

**英文释义**：to recognize the value; to be grateful

**例句**：
- I really **appreciate** your help.
- She **appreciates** classical music.

**搭配**：
- appreciate doing sth. 感激做某事
- appreciate the beauty of 欣赏……的美

**辨析**：
- appreciate "欣赏、感激"，强调理解价值
- enjoy "享受"，强调从中获得快乐
- value "重视、珍视"

**记忆法**：`ap-`(加强) + `preci`(价值，源于 price) + `-ate` → 给某物估价 → 重视、欣赏
