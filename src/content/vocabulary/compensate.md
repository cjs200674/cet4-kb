# compensate

**释义**：补偿；赔偿；弥补

**英文释义**：to pay someone for loss or damage; to make up for

**例句**：
- The company **compensated** the workers for the accident.
- Nothing can **compensate** for the loss of a loved one.

**搭配**：
- compensate for 弥补
- compensate sb. for sth. 因某事赔偿某人

**派生**：
- compensation (n.) 补偿、赔偿
- compensatory (adj.) 补偿性的

**记忆法**：`com-`(一起) + `pens`(支付，源于 expense) + `-ate` → 一起支付 → 补偿
