# promote

**释义**：促进；推广；晋升

**英文释义**：to help something develop; to advertise; to raise in rank

**例句**：
- The new policy **promotes** sustainable development.
- She was **promoted** to general manager.

**搭配**：
- promote sales 促进销售
- promote cooperation 促进合作
- promote awareness 提高意识
- be promoted to 被提升到

**派生**：
- promotion (n.) 促进、晋升
- promoter (n.) 推广者
- promotional (adj.) 推广的

**记忆法**：`pro-`(向前) + `mote`(动，源于 motion) → 向前动 → 促进、推广
