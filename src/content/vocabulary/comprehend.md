# comprehend

**释义**：理解；领悟；包含

**英文释义**：to understand something fully; to include

**例句**：
- I cannot **comprehend** his reasons.
- The park **comprehends** all kinds of entertainment.

**搭配**：
- comprehend the meaning 理解意义

**辨析**：
- comprehend "全面理解"
- understand "理解"，最通用
- grasp "抓住、领悟"

**记忆法**：`com-`(一起) + `prehend`(抓住，源于 grasp) → 全部抓住 → 理解
