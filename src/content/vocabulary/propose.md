# propose

**释义**：建议；提议；求婚

**英文释义**：to suggest; to offer marriage

**例句**：
- I **propose** that we delay the meeting.
- He **proposed** a solution to the problem.

**搭配**：
- propose doing sth. 建议做某事
- propose to sb. 向某人求婚
- propose a toast 敬酒

**派生**：
- proposal (n.) 提议、提案
- proposition (n.) 主张、命题
- proposed (adj.) 拟议的

**辨析**：
- propose "提议"，较正式
- suggest "建议"，最通用
- recommend "推荐"
- advise "劝告"

**记忆法**：`pro-`(在前) + `pose`(放) → 把想法放到前面 → 提议
