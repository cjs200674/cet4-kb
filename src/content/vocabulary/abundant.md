# abundant

**释义**：丰富的；充裕的

**英文释义**：existing in large quantities; plentiful

**例句**：
- The region has **abundant** natural resources.
- Birds are **abundant** in the forest.

**搭配**：
- abundant in 富于……的
- abundant resources 丰富的资源
- abundant evidence 充分的证据

**记忆法**：`ab-`(加强) + `und`(波浪，源于 undulate) + `-ant`(形容词后缀) → 像波浪一样多的 → 丰富的
