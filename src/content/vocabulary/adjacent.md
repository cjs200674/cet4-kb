# adjacent

**释义**：邻近的；毗连的

**英文释义**：next to or very close to something

**例句**：
- Our office is **adjacent** to the library.
- The houses are **adjacent** to each other.

**搭配**：
- adjacent to 邻近、紧挨着
- adjacent angles 邻角
- adjacent rooms 相邻的房间

**记忆法**：`ad-`(朝向) + `jac`(靠近，源于 adjacent 的词根) + `-ent` → 朝向某物靠近的
