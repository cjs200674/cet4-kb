# priority

**释义**：优先；优先事项

**英文释义**：something that is more important than other things; the right to be first

**例句**：
- Education is a top **priority** for the government.
- Safety should be our first **priority**.

**搭配**：
- top priority 头等大事
- give priority to 优先考虑
- priority list 优先事项清单
- high / low priority 高/低优先级

**派生**：
- prioritize (v.) 优先处理
- prioritization (n.) 优先排序

**记忆法**：`prior`(在前的) + `-ity` → 在前面的事物 → 优先
