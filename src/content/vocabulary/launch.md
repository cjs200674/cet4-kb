# launch

**释义**：发射；启动；推出；下水

**英文释义**：to start a project or product; to send into the air/water

**例句**：
- The company **launched** a new product last month.
- The satellite was **launched** into orbit.

**搭配**：
- launch a product 推出产品
- launch a campaign 发起运动
- launch a rocket 发射火箭
- product launch 产品发布会

**派生**：
- launcher (n.) 发射器
- relaunch (v.) 重新启动

**辨析**：
- launch "启动、推出"
- start 最通用
- initiate "开始、发起"
- begin "开始"

**记忆法**：源于 lance(长矛) → 像掷出长矛一样 → 发射
