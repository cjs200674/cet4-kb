# eliminate

**释义**：消除；排除；淘汰

**英文释义**：to remove completely; to get rid of

**例句**：
- We must **eliminate** all errors.
- She was **eliminated** in the first round.

**搭配**：
- eliminate poverty / errors 消除贫困/错误
- eliminate the need for 消除对……的需要

**派生**：
- elimination (n.) 消除、淘汰

**辨析**：
- eliminate "完全消除"
- remove "移除"，较通用
- exclude "排除在外"
- erase "擦除"

**记忆法**：`e-`(出) + `limin`(门槛，源于 limit) + `-ate` → 推出门外 → 消除
