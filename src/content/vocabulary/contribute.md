# contribute

**释义**：贡献；捐献；促成

**英文释义**：to give or donate; to help cause

**例句**：
- He **contributed** $100 to the charity.
- Various factors **contributed** to his success.

**搭配**：
- contribute to 贡献、促成
- contribute to doing sth. 有助于做某事

**派生**：
- contribution (n.) 贡献
- contributor (n.) 贡献者

**记忆法**：`con-`(一起) + `tribute`(分配，给予) → 一起给予 → 贡献
