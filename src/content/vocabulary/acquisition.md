**词性**：n

**搭配 / 词组**：

- data acquisition: 数据采集
- data acquisition system: 数据采集系统；资料获取系统
- language acquisition: 语言习得
- land acquisition: 土地征用
- merger and acquisition: 收购兼并；兼并与收购
