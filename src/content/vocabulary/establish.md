# establish

**释义**：建立；确立；证实

**英文释义**：to set up; to prove or show

**例句**：
- The company was **established** in 1990.
- The police **established** his innocence.

**搭配**：
- establish a company / relationship 建立公司/关系
- establish a reputation 树立声誉
- establish the fact 确认事实

**派生**：
- establishment (n.) 建立、机构
- established (adj.) 已确立的、著名的

**记忆法**：`e-`(出) + `stabl`(站立，源于 stable) + `-ish` → 使站立出来 → 建立
