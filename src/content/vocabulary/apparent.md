# apparent

**释义**：明显的；表面上的

**英文释义**：clearly seen or understood; seeming to be real

**例句**：
- It was **apparent** that he was unhappy.
- His **apparent** calm hid his anxiety.

**搭配**：
- apparent to 对……明显的
- apparent contradiction 明显的矛盾
- for no apparent reason 没有明显原因

**辨析**：
- apparent 强调"看起来明显"
- obvious "显然的"，更口语
- evident "明显的"，强调有证据

**记忆法**：`ap-`(朝向) + `par`(出现，源于 appear) + `-ent` → 显现出来的 → 明显的
