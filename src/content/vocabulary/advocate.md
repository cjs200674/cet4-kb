# advocate

**释义**：提倡；拥护；倡导者；律师

**英文释义**：to publicly support; a person who supports

**例句**：
- She **advocates** for environmental protection.
- He is a strong **advocate** of free education.

**搭配**：
- advocate for / of 提倡、支持
- an advocate of peace 和平的拥护者
- advocate reform 倡导改革

**记忆法**：`ad-`(朝向) + `voc`(声音，源于 vocal) + `-ate` → 大声呼吁 → 倡导
