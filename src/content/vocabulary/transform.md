# transform

**释义**：转换；转变；改变

**英文释义**：to change completely in form or appearance

**例句**：
- Technology has **transformed** the way we live.
- The city has been **transformed** into a modern metropolis.

**搭配**：
- transform ... into ... 把……变成……
- transform one's life 改变生活

**派生**：
- transformation (n.) 转变
- transformative (adj.) 变革性的

**辨析**：
- transform "完全改变"
- change 最通用
- convert "转换、改变"
- alter "改变、修改"

**记忆法**：`trans-`(跨越) + `form`(形状) → 跨形式 → 转变
