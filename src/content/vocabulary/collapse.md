# collapse

**释义**：倒塌；崩溃；瓦解

**英文释义**：to fall down suddenly; to fail completely

**例句**：
- The building **collapsed** during the earthquake.
- The negotiation **collapsed** after hours of talks.

**搭配**：
- collapse of 倒塌、崩溃
- economic collapse 经济崩溃
- mental / nervous collapse 精神/神经崩溃

**派生**：
- collapsible (adj.) 可折叠的

**记忆法**：`col-`(一起) + `lapse`(滑，落) → 一起滑落 → 倒塌
