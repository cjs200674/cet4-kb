# consecutive

**释义**：连续的；连贯的

**英文释义**：following each other without interruption

**例句**：
- It rained for **consecutive** three days.
- He won **consecutive** championships.

**搭配**：
- consecutive days 连续多日
- consecutive interpretation 同声传译(注意：同声传译其实是 simultaneous)

**辨析**：
- consecutive 强调"连续不断"
- successive "连续的、接连的"
- continuous "持续的、不间断的"

**记忆法**：`con-`(一起) + `secut`(跟随，源于 sequence) + `-ive` → 一个接一个 → 连续的
