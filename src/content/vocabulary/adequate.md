# adequate

**释义**：足够的；适当的

**英文释义**：enough in quantity or quality; satisfactory

**例句**：
- His salary is **adequate** to support his family.
- The room is small but **adequate** for our needs.

**搭配**：
- adequate for 对……足够
- adequate to do sth. 足以做某事
- adequate preparation 充分的准备

**辨析**：
- adequate 强调"够用但不一定充裕"
- enough 最常用，"足够的"
- sufficient 更正式，"充分的"
- ample "充裕的"，有富余

**记忆法**：`ad-`(朝向) + `equ`(相等，源于 equal) + `-ate` → 趋向相等的 → 足够的
