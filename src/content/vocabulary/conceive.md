# conceive

**释义**：构想；设想；怀孕

**英文释义**：to form an idea; to become pregnant

**例句**：
- She **conceived** the idea while traveling.
- The project was **conceived** three years ago.

**搭配**：
- conceive of 想象、设想
- conceive a child 怀孕

**派生**：
- concept (n.) 概念
- conception (n.) 构想、概念
- conceivable (adj.) 可想象的

**记忆法**：`con-`(一起) + `ceive`(拿，取) → 把想法一起拿出 → 构想
