# negotiate

**释义**：协商；谈判；转让

**英文释义**：to discuss terms; to arrange

**例句**：
- The two sides **negotiated** a peace treaty.
- He **negotiated** a higher salary.

**搭配**：
- negotiate with sb. 与某人谈判
- negotiate a deal / contract 谈成交易/合同
- negotiate a price 议价

**派生**：
- negotiation (n.) 谈判
- negotiator (n.) 谈判者

**记忆法**：`neg-`(不) + `oti`(空闲) + `-ate` → 谈判就是没空闲
