# obtain

**释义**：获得；得到

**英文释义**：to get or acquire something

**例句**：
- He **obtained** a master's degree in 2020.
- The information can be **obtained** from the website.

**搭配**：
- obtain a degree 获得学位
- obtain information 获取信息
- obtain permission 获得许可

**辨析**：
- obtain 较正式，"获得"
- get 最通用
- acquire 强调"经过努力获得"
- gain "获得、赢得"

**记忆法**：`ob-`(加强) + `tain`(拿，源于 attain) → 拿到 → 获得
