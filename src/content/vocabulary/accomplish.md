# accomplish

**释义**：完成；实现；达到

**英文释义**：to succeed in doing or completing something

**例句**：
- She **accomplished** her goal of finishing the marathon.
- We need to **accomplish** the task before Friday.

**搭配**：
- accomplish a task / goal 完成一项任务/目标
- accomplish nothing 一事无成
- an accomplished musician 造诣很深的音乐家

**记忆法**：`ac-`(加强) + `complish`(完成) → 完成、实现。同根词：accomplishment (n. 成就)
