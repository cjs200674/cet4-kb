# hinder

**释义**：阻碍；妨碍

**英文释义**：to delay or prevent something from happening

**例句**：
- Bad weather **hindered** the rescue operation.
- Lack of funding **hindered** the research.

**搭配**：
- hinder progress 阻碍进步
- hinder sb. from doing sth. 妨碍某人做某事

**辨析**：
- hinder "阻碍、妨碍"
- prevent "阻止、防止"，更强烈
- obstruct "阻塞、阻挠"
- impede "妨碍、阻止"

**记忆法**：`hind`(后面的) + `-er` → 在后面的 → 拖后腿 → 阻碍
