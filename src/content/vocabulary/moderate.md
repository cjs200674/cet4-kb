# moderate

**释义**：适度的；中等的；缓和

**英文释义**：average in amount or intensity; to make less extreme

**例句**：
- He has **moderate** political views.
- The weather is **moderate** today.

**搭配**：
- moderate price 适中的价格
- moderate amount 适量
- moderate exercise 适度运动

**派生**：
- moderation (n.) 适度
- moderately (adv.) 适度地
- moderator (n.) 调解人

**辨析**：
- moderate "适度的"，中性
- temperate "温和的、有节制的"
- mild "温和的、不严厉的"

**记忆法**：`moder`(适度，源于 mode) + `-ate` → 适度的
