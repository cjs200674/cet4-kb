# accurate

**释义**：准确的；精确的

**英文释义**：correct and exact in all details

**例句**：
- Her report was **accurate** and well-organized.
- The watch keeps **accurate** time.

**搭配**：
- accurate description 准确的描述
- accurate information 准确的信息
- accurate prediction 准确的预测

**派生**：
- accuracy (n.) 准确性
- accurately (adv.) 准确地
- inaccurate (adj.) 不准确的

**辨析**：
- accurate "准确的"，强调精度
- exact "精确的"，强调完全一致
- precise "精密的"，强调严格
- correct "正确的"，最通用

**记忆法**：`ac-`(朝向) + `cur`(关心，源于 care) + `-ate` → 用心做的 → 准确的
