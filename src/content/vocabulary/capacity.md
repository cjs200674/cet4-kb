# capacity

**释义**：容量；能力；身份

**英文释义**：the maximum amount something can hold; ability

**例句**：
- The stadium has a seating **capacity** of 50,000.
- She has a great **capacity** for learning.

**搭配**：
- capacity for 有……的能力
- capacity to do sth. 做某事的能力
- in one's capacity as 以……的身份
- production capacity 产能

**辨析**：
- capacity 强调"潜在的最大能力"
- ability 通用"能力"
- capability "才能、容量"

**记忆法**：`cap`(拿，握住) + `-acity` → 能拿住的量 → 容量
