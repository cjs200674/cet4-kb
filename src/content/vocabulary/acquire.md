# acquire

**释义**：获得；习得；购得

**英文释义**：to get or gain something; to learn or develop a skill

**例句**：
- She **acquired** a foreign language in just six months.
- The company **acquired** a small startup last year.

**搭配**：
- acquire a habit 养成习惯
- acquire knowledge 获得知识
- acquire a reputation 获得声誉

**辨析**：
- acquire 强调"经过努力获得"
- obtain 较正式，可指获得抽象或具体事物
- get 最通用

**记忆法**：`ac-`(加强) + `quire`(寻求，源于 query) → 不断寻求 → 获得
