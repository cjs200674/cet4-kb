# attribute

**释义**：归因于；属性；特征

**英文释义**：to consider as belonging to; a quality or feature

**例句**：
- She **attributes** her success to hard work.
- Kindness is one of his best **attributes**.

**搭配**：
- attribute sth. to sth. 把……归因于……
- attribute to 归因于

**派生**：
- attribution (n.) 归属
- attributable (adj.) 可归因的

**记忆法**：`at-`(朝向) + `tribu`(分配，给予，源于 tribute) + `-te` → 分给 → 归于
