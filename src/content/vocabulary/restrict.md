# restrict

**释义**：限制；约束

**英文释义**：to limit or control

**例句**：
- Speed is **restricted** to 30 mph in this area.
- The law **restricts** the sale of alcohol.

**搭配**：
- restrict ... to ... 把……限制在……
- be restricted to 受限于

**派生**：
- restriction (n.) 限制
- restrictive (adj.) 限制性的
- restricted (adj.) 受限的

**辨析**：
- restrict "限制"
- limit "限制、限定"
- confine "限制在范围内"
- constrain "约束、强迫"

**记忆法**：`re-`(回) + `strict`(紧) → 拉紧回去 → 限制
