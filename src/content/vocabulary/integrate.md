# integrate

**释义**：整合；使一体化；融入

**英文释义**：to combine two or more things into one; to mix in

**例句**：
- The new building **integrates** with the surrounding environment.
- Immigrants should try to **integrate** into the local community.

**搭配**：
- integrate with 与……结合
- integrate into 融入
- integrate theory with practice 理论与实践相结合

**派生**：
- integration (n.) 一体化、整合
- integrated (adj.) 综合的、整体的
- disintegrate (v.) 瓦解、碎裂

**记忆法**：`integr`(完整，源于 integer) + `-ate` → 使完整合一 → 整合
