# acknowledge

**释义**：承认；致谢；告知收悉

**英文释义**：to accept or recognize something; to express gratitude

**例句**：
- He **acknowledged** his mistake publicly.
- I **acknowledge** receipt of your letter.

**搭配**：
- acknowledge one's mistake 承认错误
- acknowledge receipt of 确认收悉
- acknowledge sb.'s help 感谢某人的帮助

**记忆法**：`ac-`(加强) + `knowledge`(知道) → 大家都知道 → 公开承认
