# 2022 年 12 月真题 - 短篇新闻（科技）

## 🎧 News 1: Space Mission

> **NASA has announced** a new mission to explore the surface of Mars. The Mars rover, named "Perseverance 2", is **scheduled to launch** in 2027. According to scientists, the mission aims to search for **signs of ancient microbial life** and collect soil samples that may be returned to Earth in the future. The project is expected to cost approximately 2.7 billion dollars, making it one of the most expensive Mars missions to date.

## 🎧 News 2: Climate Summit

> **World leaders gathered in Geneva** yesterday for the annual climate summit. The two-day meeting focused on strategies to reduce carbon emissions and **combat global warming**. In his opening speech, the UN Secretary-General urged nations to **accelerate the transition** to renewable energy. He warned that **without immediate action**, the world would face **unprecedented challenges** in the coming decades.

## 🎧 News 3: Health Research

> **A new study published in The Lancet** suggests that **regular physical activity** can significantly reduce the risk of dementia in older adults. Researchers followed over 10,000 participants aged 65 and above for a period of 12 years. **The findings indicate** that those who engaged in moderate exercise for at least 30 minutes a day were 40% less likely to develop the condition compared to their sedentary counterparts. The study highlights the importance of **maintaining an active lifestyle** in old age.

## 🎯 新闻听力解题套路

**1. 听主题（30 秒）**
- 听 Who / What / When / Where
- 立刻确定话题

**2. 抓数字**
- 听到数字立刻记：2027、2.7 billion、65、30 minutes、40%
- 数字题送分题，绝对不能错

**3. 抓官方机构**
- NASA、UN、The Lancet
- 机构名一般会原词出现

**4. 抓动词**
- announce、urge、warn
- 主旨题和细节题都考
