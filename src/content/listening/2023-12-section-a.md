# 2023 年 12 月真题 - 长对话（图书馆借书）

## 🎧 原文

**W**: Excuse me, I'd like to borrow these three books. Do I need my library card?

**M**: Yes, please. And you also need to **fill out** this form for the new books.

**W**: Sure. By the way, **how long can I keep** these books?

**M**: Usually **two weeks** for students, but for new books, only one week.

**W**: I see. And **what if** I need them for a longer time?

**M**: You can **renew them online** as long as no one else has reserved them. Just **log in to** our system and click "renew".

**W**: That sounds easy. **What are the library hours** during the exam period?

**M**: We **extend our hours** to midnight during exams. And we also have a 24-hour study room on the third floor.

**W**: That's great! One more question — **is there a fee for** late returns?

**M**: **Yes, but it's minimal** — 50 cents per day per book.

**W**: OK, thanks for the information.

**M**: You're welcome. **Here's your receipt**. **Have a nice day**.

**W**: You too. Bye.
