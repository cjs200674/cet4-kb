# 2023 年 6 月真题 - 听力篇章（远程办公）

## 🎧 原文

> The way we work has changed dramatically in recent years. **Since the global pandemic**, millions of employees have shifted from traditional offices to working from home. While this transition was initially seen as a temporary measure, it has now become a permanent feature of the modern workplace.
>
> Proponents of remote work point to several benefits. **First**, employees save considerable time and money by eliminating daily commutes. **Second**, they enjoy greater flexibility in managing their work-life balance. **Third**, studies have shown that remote workers often report higher productivity levels, with fewer distractions from office chatter and meetings.
>
> However, remote work is not without its challenges. **Many workers report feelings of isolation** and miss the spontaneous interactions that occur naturally in an office environment. Team collaboration can suffer when communication is limited to video calls and emails. **Additionally**, the boundaries between work and personal life can become blurred, leading to longer working hours and increased stress.
>
> Looking ahead, **a hybrid model** appears to be the most likely future of work. This approach combines the flexibility of remote work with the social benefits of office interaction, allowing employees to work from home part of the week while still maintaining a physical presence in the office. **As technology continues to evolve**, both employers and employees are finding creative ways to make this new model work effectively.
